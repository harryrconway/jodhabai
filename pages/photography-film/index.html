Privacy Policy

Jodha Bai Estate respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

Information We Collect

When you contact Jodha Bai Estate through our website, we may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, preferred session date and details about your enquiry.

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide our services and manage your booking.

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Discuss availability, pricing and session details
  • Arrange and manage your booking
  • Communicate with you about your session
  • Maintain appropriate business and booking records

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may disclose information to third-party service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our website, process payments, manage bookings or provide our services. These providers are expected to handle your information appropriately and securely.

How We Store Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

Information relating to enquiries and bookings may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal, accounting or record-keeping purposes.

Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.

Where appropriate, you may also request that we delete your personal information, subject to any legal or legitimate business requirements requiring us to retain it.

To make a privacy request, please contact venue@jodhabaiestate.com.

Website & Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as website analytics, booking systems, payment providers or embedded content. These services may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Where applicable, our website may also use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and understand how visitors use the site.

Privacy Act

Jodha Bai Estate aims to handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, to the extent that they apply to our business.

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first at venue@jodhabaiestate.com so we can work to resolve the matter.

Terms & Conditions

General

All photography, film and creative sessions at Jodha Bai Estate are subject to availability and are confirmed only once the required deposit or full payment has been received. All prices are quoted in Australian dollars and include GST where applicable.

Bookings & Payment

A booking is considered confirmed once the required payment has been received and confirmation has been issued by Jodha Bai Estate.

The booking covers the agreed date, time, areas of the Estate and number of guests, cast and crew specified at the time of booking. Any changes must be approved by management prior to the session.

Cancellations

Cancellations made more than 30 days before the scheduled session will receive a full refund, less any applicable third-party booking or processing fees.

Cancellations made within 30 days of the session are non-refundable.

We strongly recommend that all productions obtain appropriate cancellation, public liability and equipment insurance.

Changes & Rescheduling

Requests to change a confirmed booking date or session details are subject to availability and approval by Jodha Bai Estate.

Where Jodha Bai Estate is unable to honour a confirmed booking due to circumstances beyond our reasonable control, we will offer either a full refund or the opportunity to reschedule.

Property & Damage

The booking party is responsible for the conduct of all guests, vendors, cast and crew attending the Estate.

Any loss or damage to the property, buildings, furnishings, gardens or other Estate items caused during the booking may be charged to the booking party.

The Estate must be returned in the condition in which it was found, allowing for reasonable wear and use.

Liability

Jodha Bai Estate accepts no liability for loss, injury, theft or damage to persons, vehicles, equipment or personal property occurring during a session, except where liability cannot lawfully be excluded.

Guests, vendors, cast and crew enter and use the property at their own risk and are responsible for taking reasonable care while on the Estate.

Approvals & Use of the Estate

Commercial filming, drone operation, significant furniture movement, additional crew or vehicles, and any activity outside the agreed booking scope require prior approval from management.

Jodha Bai Estate reserves the right to refuse or stop any activity that may cause damage to the property, create unreasonable risk or breach these terms.

Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia. Any disputes arising in connection with a booking will be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of New South Wales, to the extent permitted by law.

Jodha Bai Estate Rules

A few things to know.

We want your time at Jodha Bai Estate to feel effortless, creative and entirely your own. A little care ensures the Estate remains beautiful for you, and for those who come after you.

Respect the Estate

Please treat the architecture, furnishings, gardens and grounds with care. The booking party is responsible for any damage caused during the session.

Inside the Residence

If shoes are wet or soiled, please remove them before entering the residence interiors. Styled areas, props and furnishings must not be moved without prior approval from management.

Sound & Neighbours

Outdoor music and amplified sound must finish by 10 pm, out of respect for our neighbouring properties.

Arrival & Production

Vendors, crew and production teams must confirm bump-in and bump-out times, along with final cast and crew numbers, with management prior to arrival.

Parking

On-site parking is available for up to 10 vehicles. Non-essential vehicles should be parked on the street.

Smoking, Drones & Filming

Smoking is permitted only in designated outdoor areas. Drone use and commercial filming require prior written approval from management.

Pets

Pets are welcome by prior arrangement.

Make Yourself at Home.

We want your session to feel relaxed, unhurried and entirely your own. Our aim is simple: to give you the space, privacy and setting to create something extraordinary.
