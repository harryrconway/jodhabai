Privacy Policy

Jodha Bai Estate collects personal information, including your name, email address and phone number, only when you voluntarily submit an enquiry or make a booking through our website or via third party booking platforms. We do not sell, trade or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties.

Information you provide is used solely to respond to your enquiry, process your booking and improve the guest experience. We may retain correspondence for up to 24 months to assist with future stays. All data is stored securely and access is restricted to retreat management only.

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise this right, please contact us at venue@jodhabaiestate.com. This policy is governed by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Jodha Bai Estate Rules

To ensure every photography, film and creative session runs smoothly, please observe the following.

  • No smoking inside any building on the property. Designated outdoor areas are provided.
  • Please remove shoes before entering the residence interiors. Slippers are provided on request.
  • A noise curfew applies after 10 pm for outdoor music and amplified sound, out of respect for neighbouring properties.
  • Vendors, crew and production teams must confirm bump in and bump out times in advance with management.
  • Please treat the property, furnishings and grounds with care. Bookings are responsible for any damage caused during their session.
  • Styled areas, props and furnishings must not be moved between rooms without prior approval.
  • Parking is available on site; please follow the signage and any directions given on the day.
  • Drone use and commercial filming require prior written approval from management.
  • Pets are welcome by prior arrangement only.

We want every session here to feel considered, unhurried and entirely your own. Thank you for helping us keep it that way.
