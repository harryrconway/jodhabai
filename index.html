About Jodha Bai Estate

A 450 year old palace,
rebuilt by hand.

In 1571, the Mughal Emperor Akbar built a palace at Fatehpur Sikri for his beloved wife, Jodha Bai.

More than four centuries later, in 2010, Cliff and Susan Peiffer stood beneath its red sandstone arches and imagined bringing it back to life.

On a hillside in Terranora, New South Wales, that vision became a seven-year labour of devotion. Working as owner-builders alongside their designer, they recreated each column, window and carved detail by hand, faithful to the spirit and craftsmanship of the original.

300 tonnes of Indian sandstone. Seven years of craftsmanship. One remarkable estate.

Today, Jodha Bai Estate is a private world unlike anything else in the region, reserved for one gathering at a time.
